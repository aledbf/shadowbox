# Known, understood log lines that must not fail a run.
#
# One extended regular expression per line, matched against the whole log
# line.  Every entry needs a comment saying why it is here and what would
# have to happen for it to be deleted -- an allowlist without reasons
# decays into a way of not looking at the logs.
#
# "Unsupported" is a legitimate result.  Noise is not.

# --- the PVM backend's declared gaps ------------------------------------
# kvm_x86_vendor_init() and kvm_pmu_ops_update() WARN once per kvm_x86_ops,
# kvm_x86_nested_ops and kvm_pmu_ops entry that the vendor module leaves
# NULL.  PVM implements no nested virtualisation and no PMU on purpose.
# Delete these when those gaps close, not before.
WARNING: .*kvm-x86-ops\.h:[0-9]+ at kvm_x86_vendor_init\+
WARNING: .*kvm-x86-nested-ops\.h:[0-9]+ at kvm_x86_vendor_init\+
WARNING: .*kvm-x86-pmu-ops\.h:[0-9]+ at kvm_pmu_ops_update\+

# --- lines that only quote a pattern ------------------------------------
# The sanitizer's own report, and the test harness naming a case, both
# contain the words they are looking for.
^(known|fail|  )?.*sanitize-log:
^(ok|not ok) [0-9]+ - 
^#
